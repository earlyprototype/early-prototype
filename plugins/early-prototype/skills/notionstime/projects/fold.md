# fold

*A Deleuzian methodology lab for LLM experiments.*

**Repo**: github.com/earlyprototype/fold
**Status**: Early-stage methodological substrate. Conceptual scaffolding established; specific experiments emerging.
**Scale in the programme**: Methodological substrate — the framework from which other projects emerge.

---

## What it is

A digital prototyping lab for LLM experiments, framed on the surface as inspired by Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein* — the seam between making and what gets made, the cost of creating something whose nature is not fully grasped.

The conceptual scaffolding underneath the surface framing is Deleuze and Guattari's *A Thousand Plateaus*. The directory structure follows it directly:

- **SEED** — germination, becoming
- **SMOOTHSPACE** — the undifferentiated, possibility-rich
- **STRIATEDSPACE** — gridded, structured, constraining
- **MECHINIPHYLUM** — the machinic phylum, lineages of technical objects

This is the methodological substrate from which other projects in the programme emerge. The Deleuzian framework runs through Thom's thinking deliberately.

---

## What question it addresses

How does the methodology of experimenting with LLMs shape what those experiments can reveal? What working frameworks let cognition operate productively in territory it has not previously mapped?

---

## Method or approach

The fold framework treats LLM experiments as movements through smooth and striated space. Striated space provides structure (the schema, the protocol, the established categories). Smooth space provides possibility (the unmapped territory, the situations existing frames cannot account for). Productive experimentation lives in the tension between them — between the constraint of structure and the openness of what structure cannot yet hold.

ATR emerged from this framework as an experiment in dissolving the striated space of normal generation to reveal the smooth-space substrate of weight geometry.

## Caveats Thom has named

- Early-stage. The scaffolding is established but the specific experiments emerging from it are still developing.
- Methodological rather than empirical. The framework guides experiments; the empirical claims live in the projects that emerge from it.

## Connections to other projects in the programme

**Established:**

- ATR sits inside fold's methodology. ATR's reference to Deleuze's Body without Organs as the undifferentiated substrate (weight geometry before prompt input) directly invokes fold's framework.

- The cognitive prosthetic architecture's relationship to operational reality maps onto smooth/striated dynamics. The graph's schema striates cognitive space. The diff catches when the striation is at odds with the smooth space of operational reality. The realisation interface lets the agent navigate between striated structure and unmapped territory. This isn't decorative — the Deleuzian frame names what the architecture does.

**Emergent:**

- fold's framework may be the explicit philosophical grounding the cognitive prosthetic architecture work has been operating from without naming. Worth surfacing in the architecture's writing.
